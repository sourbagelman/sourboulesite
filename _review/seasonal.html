The separate 2027 year mark

Production shows 2027 from January 1 through February 28, 2027 in Chicago, then removes it on March 1. It does not appear automatically in December 2026 or later years. Hiding the year never disables seasonal decorations.

The countdown, registration, cookie passes, and staff redemption are outside this integration.
