Good questions welcome

Frequently asked questions.

What to expect, how to prepare and how to protect the results of your treatment.

What should I expect with my first facial?

We’ll discuss your overall skin goals, evaluate your skin and determine the best course of action. Each treatment includes exfoliation, extractions and relaxing massage, and the goal is for you to leave with hydrated, healthy skin.

What products do you use?

Lira Clinical and Skin Script products are used in the treatment room. Both lines were selected with ingredients and budget in mind and incorporate plant- and fruit-derived ingredients.

How can I maintain results between treatments?

There is no one-size-fits-all plan. Professional skincare products are available and can be tailored to your needs. Appropriate home care and continued treatments help maintain results.

What is a chemical peel?

A chemical solution is applied to the treatment area to exfoliate the skin. The specific peel is chosen after a skin analysis. Peels may address:

  • Sun damage and melasma
  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Acne and related scars
  • Uneven tone and texture
What will my skin look like after a peel?

You may experience redness and tightness immediately afterward. Some clients have light shedding while others experience heavier peeling. Skin may look worse before it looks better, and blemishes can surface during a temporary purging phase. The full result can take weeks or months to develop. A recommended home-care regimen supports the best outcome.

How important is sunscreen?

SPF is the most important part of a skincare regimen. A mineral sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher is recommended every day, including cloudy days. Zinc oxide is a preferred ingredient.

Can I get a facial while pregnant or breastfeeding?

Yes. The facial will be customized for relaxation and hydration, while certain ingredients and treatment methods will be omitted.

How often should I get a facial?

Facials may be scheduled weekly, monthly or whenever suits your lifestyle and budget. Corrective treatments may be performed more frequently.

What is dermaplaning?

Dermaplaning uses a surgical scalpel to gently remove dead skin and fine vellus hair from the surface. It should not hurt, and vellus hair does not grow back coarser or darker.

Can I wear makeup after a facial, microdermabrasion or dermaplaning?

Yes, but if possible, allow the skin to breathe for 24 hours and benefit from the products used during your service.

Can I schedule a service after Botox or filler?

Schedule the skincare service before your injection appointment or at least two weeks afterward.

I’m on Accutane. What can I have done?

No services are performed while a client is taking Accutane. You must be off the prescription for six months before treatment.

I have acne. What treatment is best?

An acne-focused facial can help detoxify pores and address bacteria. This option may be customized for teens age 14 and older as well as adults experiencing acne. A consultation can identify the current best-fit service.
