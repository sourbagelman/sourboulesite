Skin Classic

A precise, non-laser treatment for common minor skin irregularities, priced after a consultation based on the amount of work requested.

What is Skin Classic?

Skin Classic uses high-frequency technology to dehydrate the top layer of a skin irregularity, allowing new, healthy skin to emerge. Treatment is quick, precise and effective.

What can it treat?

  • Broken capillaries and flat hyperpigmentation
  • Skin tags, milia and clogged pores
  • Blackheads, whiteheads and active acne
  • Cholesterol deposits and cherry angiomas
  • Sebaceous hyperplasia and seborrheic keratosis

Healing & downtime

The treated area may be red and inflamed immediately afterward. Within 24–48 hours, a scab-like crust typically forms and falls away on its own in 7–14 days. Swelling, bruising or pinpoint bleeding may occur depending on the treatment area.

After the crust falls away, skin may look pinkish or brownish for six to eight weeks as the healing cycle continues.

Please do not pick. Until scabs release naturally, use a gentle cleanser, moisturizer and SPF. Avoid submerging treated areas and keep excess sweat away from them.

Makeup & home care

Mineral makeup may be used during healing, but full coverage is not recommended. If makeup is necessary, remove it very gently. Corrective topicals—including tretinoin and high-percentage AHA/BHA products—should be avoided for approximately seven days before treatment and until scabs have fallen.

What does it feel like?

The probe creates a brief stinging sensation without piercing the skin. The current site compares it to an ant bite and notes that this superficial process keeps 15-minute treatments tolerable.

Who is not a candidate?

The current site excludes clients with pacemakers, pregnancy, breastfeeding, lupus, Accutane use, diabetes, an active cold sore, melasma, moles or another dermatological condition outside the provider’s scope.

Why not melasma?

Melasma is a hormonal form of pigmentation induced by heat. Because Skin Classic uses heat, it may worsen melasma.

Why not moles?

Moles have roots and should be evaluated and removed by a dermatologist.

At a glance

  • Price: starts at $75
  • Length: based on treatment area
  • Crusting: about 7–14 days
  • Consultation: required
