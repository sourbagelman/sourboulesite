Refresh + brighten

GetGlowing Micro-Peel

A lighter Elaine Brennan peel designed to refine texture, brighten discoloration and refresh the skin with less downtime than the full Skin Renewal Peel.

How it works

A cream is applied to encourage exfoliation of dead skin cells and debris while supporting the skin’s natural renewal process. The treatment is tailored to your skin type and long-term goals.

It may help smooth fine lines, even discoloration, improve texture and reduce the appearance of acne scars and environmental damage. Improvements may build with each procedure.

Treatment plan & downtime

The current recommendation for maximum results is a series of six procedures spaced two to six weeks apart, depending on skin type. The peel portion takes approximately 45 minutes and may be added to a facial or deep-cleansing service.

For about one week, skin undergoes a renewal process. Peeling may begin on days two through four and last about three days. Mild itching, dryness and redness may continue for 7–10 days.

Active ingredients

Resorcinol

Used to break down rough, scaly or hardened skin and address acne, photo-aging, tone and unevenness.

Kojic acid

An antioxidant that interferes with melanin production and is used for skin brightening.

Salicylic acid

An exfoliant used for oily skin, acne, blackheads, whiteheads and inflammation.

Who should not receive this peel?

  • Clients with herpes simplex, cold sores or fever blisters unless taking antiviral medication
  • Severe cold or flu symptoms
  • Allergy to resorcinol or salicylic acid/aspirin
  • Open wounds or lesions
  • Pregnancy or nursing
  • Accutane use within the past 6–12 months
  • Sunburn, allergy or tanning-bed use
  • Cancer treatment, radiation or autoimmune disease
Sun care matters. This treatment can increase sun sensitivity. Use adequate UVA/UVB sunscreen during treatment and for at least one month afterward, including on cloudy winter days.

Additional precautions

Avoid depilatory creams and facial waxing for seven days before the peel. Stinging, peeling, flaking, irritation, itching, redness or mild swelling may occur. Do not pick or pull at peeling skin because doing so can lead to scabbing or scarring.

  • Suggested series: six
