Cancellation policy

Your business means so much to The Beauty Builder. To schedule an appointment, clients must provide a credit card to be kept securely on file.

Please notify The Beauty Builder promptly of scheduling conflicts. Appointment changes must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment to avoid a charge of at least $25 or 50% of all scheduled services, whichever is greater.

No-shows are charged 100% of the service. This policy is part of the agreement made with the business and protects the provider’s ability to serve every client.
