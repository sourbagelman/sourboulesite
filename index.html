To our Sour Boule community

Since opening The Sour Boule, we’ve spent every holiday season doing what we love: feeding people.

We’ve helped fill your family’s tables, been part of your traditions, and opened our doors for free Thanksgiving meals so anyone who needed a meal or a place to belong had somewhere to go.

We’re proud of that. Your trust has made those moments possible, and being part of your lives has meant more to us than we can put into words.

This year, though, we’re taking a little time to be with the people who have shared so much of us with everyone else.

Running a small, family-owned business takes more than long hours. It means early mornings, late nights, missed gatherings, and children who patiently wait for you to finish “just one more thing.” Behind every meal we’ve made is a family that has given up time together to help us make it happen.

Our children have grown through these busy years, and there are moments we can’t get back. This Thanksgiving, we want to slow down, cook alongside them, sit at the same table, and stay a little longer. We want to give our families the time and presence they’ve missed—and that we’ve missed, too.

The Sour Boule will be closed for the Thanksgiving holiday break, reopening Wednesday, December 2.

Holiday preorders

Already placed an order?

We’ll be reaching out to you by Monday evening, November 23, to arrange pickup or delivery of your order. We’ll make sure you have a plan for getting your holiday goodies.

Online ordering is paused during our holiday break.

Thank you for making us part of your traditions and for understanding as we take this time for our own. We hope your Thanksgiving brings good food, a little rest, and time with the people you love.

With love and gratitude,
The Sour Boule family
